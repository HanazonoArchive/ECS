How to Use ECS

1

Select Your Game

Click a game chip from the top bar to load the correct energy system. Drag to scroll through all profiles.

Genshin HSR WuWa
2

Enter Your Current Energy

Type your current energy amount into the input field, or drag the slider. Use the quick buttons (+10, +20, +40, MAX, CLR) for speed.

120 |
3

Read Your Results

The telemetry panel instantly shows when your energy will be full (in 12-hour AM/PM format), the countdown timer, and recovery milestones.

Full at: 08:30 PM
4

Target Time Solvers

Set a desired time (e.g. 8:00 PM). ECS tells you how much energy to spend now so you'll cap exactly at that time, or forecasts how much you'll have by then.
